### CampusPay — IoT contactless campus payments

- One-line: An IoT-powered prototype that turns a regular RFID-enabled student ID card into a universal campus payment tool. Tagline: "One card. Every campus need."
- Why: campus payments suffered from cash handling, UPI failures on bad campus networks, and juggling separate cards and apps at every mess queue and store counter.
- Built end to end: physical RFID hardware, a Django / Django REST backend, a live web dashboard, and machine learning models underneath.
- Hardware: Arduino UNO R4 WiFi with an MFRC522 RFID reader and an OLED display that shows "CampusPay — Scan Card", then a green "SUCCESS! Student: Ravish" message after payment.
- Flow: student logs into a dashboard (mess credits, campus credits, recent activity), browses unified outlets (mess, canteen, stationery, juice bar), orders an item, confirms, taps the ID card on the physical terminal, and credits are deducted instantly and reflected on the dashboard. No typed PIN, no app switching.
- Payments model: dual wallet separating "Mess Credits" (meals) from "Campus Credits" (everything else); tap-to-eat in the mess, order-then-tap in stores; cross-campus interoperability built into the architecture.
- Machine learning: a Random Forest Regressor forecasts daily mess demand from attendance patterns, exam schedules, festivals and menu trends to cut food waste; an Isolation Forest plus time-series anomaly detection layer watches transaction patterns (skipped meals, irregular timings, reduced activity) for early signs of burnout or isolation and surfaces anonymised alerts to counsellors, never raw student data.
- Recognition: 1st Prize at IoT Sensex. Published on his portfolio in June 2026.
